Our Services

From tracking down a problem to rebuilding and testing parts, we handle your diesel engines and generators.

Diagnostics & Repair

We find the root cause and fix it right, on marine diesel engines of any make.

  • Troubleshooting and diagnostics
  • Repairs, from small leaks to major failures
  • Top-end overhauls: cylinder heads and valves

Component Rebuilds & Dyno Testing

Worn parts rebuilt to spec, then tested so you know they perform.

  • Injectors and injection pumps
  • Raw water and coolant pumps
  • Turbochargers and cylinder heads
  • Alternators and starter motors
  • Dyno testing to verify power and performance

Diesel Generators

Sales, installation, testing, and repair for all types of diesel generators.

  • New generator sales and installation
  • Load testing under full power
  • Troubleshooting and repair for any make or model

Surveys & Sea Trials

Know exactly what condition your engines and generators are in.

  • Engine and generator surveys, including pre-purchase
  • Sea trials to confirm everything runs right on the water

Routine Maintenance

Service on the manufacturer's schedule, to prevent breakdowns and extend engine life.

  • Scheduled service at the recommended hour intervals
  • Oil and filter changes
  • Belts, hoses, and impellers
  • Pre-trip inspections
